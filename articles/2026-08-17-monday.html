The Bond Market Takes the Wheel

Stocks sit a whisker from records, but the story this week isn't equities — it's the long end of the Treasury curve, where a global sell-off and a re-heating oil market are quietly setting the terms for everything else.

James Thomas MinnehanMarkets — The Monday Outlook
Mon · Aug 17, 2026

The S&P 500 opened the week doing what it has done for a month — hovering just under a record — and then slipped 0.5% to 7,745.06 as rising oil prices renewed the pressure on inflation. But the index level is a distraction. The real action is in the bond market, where long-dated yields are climbing toward levels not seen in nearly two decades and a global sell-off is lifting borrowing costs from Ottawa to Tokyo. That is the story to watch this week.

Why the curve is in charge

When the 30-year Treasury yield pushes toward its highest since 2007, it stops being a bond-desk footnote and becomes the gravity that pulls on everything. Higher long rates raise the cost of capital for the AI build-out, pressure equity valuations, and lift the government's own interest bill. The sell-off isn't really about growth — it is about supply, deficits, and a wall of long-dated corporate issuance competing with Washington for buyers. That is a structural problem, and structural problems don't resolve in a single session.

Layered on top is oil. Crude is firming again on renewed uncertainty around the Strait of Hormuz, and every dollar higher feeds straight into the inflation expectations that are keeping the long end pinned. Monday's dip in stocks was the market acknowledging both at once.

You can ignore the bond market for a while. You cannot ignore it for long. This is one of those weeks it insists on being heard.The Monday Outlook
The outlook

Equities are near records but no longer in the driver's seat. Watch the 30-year yield and the oil tape — they will dictate whether stocks hold their highs. Retail earnings offer a consumer check midweek, and the countdown to Warsh's Jackson Hole keynote next Friday now overshadows everything.
